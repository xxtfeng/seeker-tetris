# Privacy Policy for Seeker Tetris

**Last Updated: 2026-02-28**

Seeker Tetris is a decentralized application (dApp) built for the Solana Seeker. We value your privacy.

### 1. Information Collection
- **Wallet Address**: We only access your public Solana wallet address when you click "Connect Wallet" to track your TET COINS rewards.
- **No Personal Data**: We do NOT collect names, emails, or any private keys.

### 2. How Data is Used
- Your wallet address is used solely to display your in-game progress and rewards on the leaderboard.

### 3. Third-Party Services
- This dApp is hosted on Vercel. Solana network interactions are handled via your connected wallet provider (e.g., Phantom or Solflare).
